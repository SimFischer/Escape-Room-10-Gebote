Create an original hand-drawn 2D cartoon adventure game environment with strong irregular dark ink outlines, painterly cel shading, quirky slightly crooked architecture, rich teal, turquoise, burnt orange wood, golden yellow, coral and mint palette. Warm welcoming whimsical mood appropriate for age 10-12.
