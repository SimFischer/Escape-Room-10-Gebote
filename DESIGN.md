Die Gestaltung greift die warme, gezeichnete Atmosphäre eines klassischen Point-and-Click-Adventures auf. Das vom Nutzer gezeigte Deponia-Bild diente als gestalterische Richtung; es ist nicht Bestandteil der Webseite. Die Kapellenkulisse ist eine eigenständige, neu erzeugte Illustration.

Bilddatei: `assets/kapelle.png`.
Erzeugung: integrierte Bildgenerierung (ImageGen), kein CLI-Fallback.

## Verwendeter Bildprompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide background illustration for an actual web point-and-click educational adventure called Die Regenbogen-Kapelle. Create an original hand-drawn 2D cartoon adventure game environment with strong irregular dark ink outlines, painterly cel shading, quirky slightly crooked architecture, rich teal, turquoise, burnt orange wood, golden yellow, coral and mint palette. Warm welcoming whimsical mood appropriate for age 10-12. Scene: interior of a cozy tiny fantasy chapel converted into an exploration workshop, large arched stained glass window at upper center-right, warm sunlight, wooden beams, bookshelves, old books and scroll on a small table at left, carved double door at right, potted green plants, tools and colored glass pieces on right workbench, warm terracotta and cream tiled floor. No people. Wide 16:9 composition. Plenty of beautiful detail at edges, center floor comparatively uncluttered so a webpage can overlay text panels. Hand-painted adventure game background reminiscent of early 2010s European comic point-and-click games such as Deponia, but a distinct original chapel environment, not that game's scene or characters. Bright daylight, not gloomy. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no interface, no UI panels or buttons baked into the image. High-quality finished illustration.
